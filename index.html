from mcp.server.fastmcp import FastMCP

mcp = FastMCP("Nobi-Interpreter")

@mcp.tool()
async def get_flight_status(flight_number: str) -> dict:
    """Fetch real-time flight departure, arrival, and delay metrics."""
    # Queries live aviation telemetry
    return {"flight": flight_number, "status": "DELAYED", "delay_minutes": 150}

@mcp.tool()
async def draft_message(recipient: str, message_intent: str, delay_info: str) -> dict:
    """Draft empathetic message for human approval before sending."""
    return {"recipient": recipient, "body": f"My flight is delayed by {delay_info}. Arriving late.", "risk_level": "medium"}
🛡️ Section 9: Safety Architecture

Human-in-the-Loop Safety Model

Nobi doesn't blindly execute every action. Actions are categorized by risk level to guarantee user control.

🟢 LOW RISK Auto-Execute

Automatic Notifications

Informational status updates, price drop alerts, and personal notifications that carry zero external side effects.

Example: "Send push notification when price drops below $300."
🟡 MEDIUM RISK Approval Required

Prepared Draft & Confirmation

External messages and emails sent to third parties (clients, hosts, sellers). The AI drafts the exact wording and asks for 1-tap confirmation.

Example: "Draft follow-up email to client regarding unpaid invoice."
🔴 HIGH RISK Strict 2FA Gate

High-Impact / Financial

Financial transfers, booking cancellations, or critical account changes. Never automatically dispatched without explicit authorization.

Example: "Cancel non-refundable flight and transfer balance."
